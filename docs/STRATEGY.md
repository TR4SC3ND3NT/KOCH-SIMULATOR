# Strategy (human overview)

**Phases**
0. Setup: repo, LFS, UE 5.8.3, C++ project, plugins, MCP.
1. Walkable blockout:
   - 1:1 campus from OSM (R1)
   - player, interaction and weapon prototype (R3)
   - placeholder characters (R2)
   - menu/settings shell (R4)
2. Vertical slice: Block C ground floor (hall, corridor, stairs, classroom, WC) at final quality, with physics and one combat encounter, profiled on both machines.
3. Campus exteriors: modular facade kits, garden, square, gate, street; lighting.
4. Story: quests, dialogue, characters, encounters (after canon lands).
5. Polish and optimization, packaging.

**Asset sources**
- Buildings: modular kits (Blender MCP + PCG).
- Props: AI from photos (Meshy/Tripo/Hunyuan3D) + cleanup, or free Fab/Poly Haven.
- Characters: MetaHuman + video mocap.
