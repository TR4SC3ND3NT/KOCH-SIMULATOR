**Route:** street (bus stop side) → zebra crossing → gate checkpoint → alley between B and A → yard in front of Block C → Block C hall.
**Story basis:** the "fired from KOCH" opening agreed on 2026-11-25 (`docs/decisions.md`, lore question #2). Story text is written by the lore author; every line marked `TODO_LORE` waits for them.

| Time | Beat | What the player does / sees | Cut first if late |

**The one beat that must not go:** the guard confrontation at the checkpoint. Without it there is no prologue.

**Out of the slice** (guide cut list, in this order): story and quests beyond the guard; face-check turnstiles; weapons and items beyond the gun and one tool; the garden and every area off this route; scan-based MetaHumans if the test head fails (stylized placeholders then); open-world and sandbox features.
