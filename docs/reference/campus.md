# Campus reference facts (INTERNAL, never in-game)

The real campus that the KOCH HQ map is built from. In-game, everything is rebranded to KOCH (AGENTS.md → Lore): the university name, logos, plaques, the college sign, the "GATEWAY TO THE WORLD" slogan and the street names are all replaced.

Raw site captures (photos/videos) are **not** in the repo: they contain people. The map owner keeps them locally (`~/alatoo-capture/`). Share selected, people-free reference frames through PRs or a shared drive if needed.

## Location and extent
- **Address:** Ala-Too International University, Bishkek, Ankara St 1/8 (A block) and 1/10 (D, C blocks). The IT & Business College is in C block.
- **A block reference point:** 42.85619 N, 74.68122 E.
- **Campus:** about 430 m E–W × 210 m N–S, 4.46 ha (OSM way 341105734).
- **Borders:**
  - South: Ankara St (4 lanes, trolleybus wires). The main gate and checkpoint are here, with a pedestrian crossing.
  - West: Cholpon-Ata St.
  - North: a service road with an industrial zone beyond.
  - East: an internal service road.
- **Terrain:** about 805 m ASL. It falls about 4–6 m to the north (≈2.7%) and about 2.5 m to the east, so there are terraces with outdoor stairs and ramps.
- **No Google Photorealistic 3D Tiles** exist for Bishkek. The sources are OSM footprints (accurate within a few metres), 2GIS floor counts, and 2020 Esri 0.31 m imagery as a tracing reference.
- Attribution required: "© OpenStreetMap contributors" (ODbL).

## Map owner notes (2026-09-24)
- **A:** 3 floors, except its left part as seen from the square, which holds the Konya Hall and the library. That part is 2 storeys, probably with double-height interiors.
- **D:** 4 floors (panoramas).
- **E:** a separate building behind the sports hall, not joined to D. It is most likely greybox `BLD_W1` (≈3 floors); to confirm. The large ex-ruin complex south of D is not E.
- **C west side:** a mono-pitch (shed) structure, high where it meets C and sloping down toward the sports hall. It is visible in the capture videos.
- **Sports area (for the site phase, not modelled yet):** left to right, one large football pitch, then an area of the same size split into 3 courts: basketball, volleyball and an empty hard court. A green steel mesh fence runs around the full perimeter and between the courts.

## Measured and estimated dimensions (±5%)
- Classroom door, clear opening ≈ 0.90–0.95 m × 2.00–2.05 m. Outer frame ≈ 1.05 × 2.10 m.
  - Single leaf, black aluminium frame, two glass panels, opens outward into the corridor.
- Classroom floor to ceiling beam ≈ 3.0 m. Light switch ≈ 0.85–0.9 m.
- Corridor ceiling ≈ 3.5 m (classroom lower at ≈3.0 m because of the beam/suspended ceiling). Floor-to-floor ≈ 3.8–4.0 m (estimate).

## Material palette
**Exteriors**
- Walls: beige render, maroon/red pilasters and window frames, red "brick" tile plinth.
- Canopies: terracotta soffits, brown pitched canopy roofs.
- Paving:
  - grey herringbone concrete pavers
  - hexagon/"clover" pavers on the street side
  - circle-pattern pavers at A block
  - diamond-pattern tiles in the garden.
- Edging: red concrete lawn edging, yellow-painted curbs, scalloped concrete edging.
- Garden furniture: wrought-iron benches with wooden slats.

**Block C interior**
- Floors: terrazzo floor and granite (red-brown speckled) stair treads.
- Walls: white walls, periwinkle-blue accent panels, black aluminium doors with glass, white steel balustrades.
- Fittings: radiators, 600×600 grid ceilings with LED panels, vending and coffee machines, wooden benches with black seats.

**Classroom (computer lab)**
- Walls: white, with a sage-green front wall carrying a diagonal orange stripe.
- Windows: PVC tilt-turn with white roller blinds.
- Furniture and floor: white desks, black mesh chairs, wood-look laminate, brown skirting.

**WC**
- Walls and floor: grey large-format porcelain tile to ≈1.4 m, white paint above, light-grey floor tile.
- Fittings: black stall doors, squat toilets with high cisterns, pedestal sinks.
