# KOCH-SIMULATOR — Lore Document

*Midterm submission, "Composition in Multimedia" · Story-driven first-person action game (Unreal Engine 5.8) · Version of 2026-11-05*

---

## How to read this document

Every paragraph, bullet and table row carries one tag that says how settled it is:

- **[CANON]** is approved by the human lore author and stated in the team's canon summary. It will not change without the author.
- **[FLAVOR]** is a team proposal: plot, names, texture and connective tissue that make the canon playable. It can still change.
- **[OPEN]** is an unresolved question, numbered as in the team's open-questions log (`#n`).

> **Canon vs flavor — one example**
>
> **[CANON]** At the gate checkpoint the turnstile refuses the hero because he is wearing a mask. A guard comes out and questions him.
>
> **[FLAVOR]** That turnstile is a *FaceGate*, the public face of KOCH's secret Project SIGHT. It logs every face that passes and has already stored the hero's bare face from a visit two weeks earlier. That is why he must wear the mask, and why the mask gets him caught.
>
> **[OPEN #3]** What the mask actually looks like (half-face, balaclava or medical) is still undecided.

## 1. Logline and premise

**[FLAVOR]** *A masked street musician walks into the headquarters of the corporation that "lost" his younger brother, with a rifle in his guitar case. He has one morning to find him before KOCH signs a contract that puts every face in the city under its watch.*

**[CANON]** The world is the headquarters campus of **KOCH**, an evil corporation. The hero is an ordinary long-haired guy who carries a **guitar case with a gun hidden inside**. His goal is to take down the evil KOCH company and its people.

**[FLAVOR]** The hero is **Arsen (Arsen)**, 26, a street guitarist and former KOCH audio-visual contractor. His brother **Isa**, 19, an unpaid intern in KOCH's Talent Program, vanished three weeks ago. KOCH says he "resigned and left the city". In fact Isa found out what the interns' work is for, hid the proof, and is now sedated in the company clinic under a Security hold.

**[FLAVOR]** The whole game is one continuous day, *Day 0*, running from about 06:40 (the alarm clock) to about 12:15 (a live broadcast). At noon the CEO signs the city-wide "Safe City" contract on stage.

| Aspect | Description | Tag |

**[FLAVOR]** **Core fantasy: "the one place that watches everyone, and one guy it can't see."** Most games give you a disguise. This one gives you a mask that hides you from the machine but makes you obvious to every human. You are an ordinary man, not a soldier, armed with a guitar case, a pepper spray, a stolen radio and the memory of having once wired the building's speakers.

## 3. The world

### 3.1 The city

**[CANON]** The game starts in the city around the campus: the hero's home, a street bus stop, a bus ride and the real road crossing in front of the campus.

**[FLAVOR]** The city is an unnamed post-Soviet Central Asian capital: trolleybus wires, nine-storey Soviet apartment blocks, marshrutka-style buses, dust and poplars. A four-lane avenue runs past the HQ. Most faces are Central Asian, some are Slavic, and the CEO is a foreigner.


**[OPEN #2]** Season and time of day are not fixed. The proposal assumes a clear early-autumn morning.

### 3.2 KOCH

**[CANON]** KOCH is an evil corporation. Its headquarters campus has blocks A, B, C and D, a garden, a square and a gate, and all of them are KOCH departments and offices. **It is not a university.**

**[FLAVOR]** In public KOCH sells "smart city" security (FaceGate turnstiles, CCTV analytics) and HR/talent services (recruiting, the Talent Program). Its private security force is **KOCH Secure**, with checkpoint guards, patrols, a tactical response team and the head of security's own elite squad.

**[OPEN #15]** Does "KOCH" stand for anything? The proposal keeps it as a brand whose letters are never explained. No character is called Koch.

### 3.3 Project SIGHT, the secret

**[FLAVOR]** SIGHT is the real product behind the friendly brochure. It is built in five layers:

| Layer | What it is | Tag |
|---|---|---|
| FaceGate | The public product: turnstiles that open by face. Every pass is stored. | **[FLAVOR]** |
| Annotation Cells | Talent Program interns tag faces from city cameras: 400 faces an hour, unpaid, called "training". | **[FLAVOR]** |
| Baseline recording | Every training room has a camera. The interns are filmed so the system can learn to predict stress, lying and "agitation". | **[FLAVOR]** |
| Risk profiles | Every identified citizen gets a tier: **Green / Watch / Refer**. "Refer" means passed to city police for detention review. | **[FLAVOR]** |
| City contract | Signed today at noon: SIGHT on every public camera, profiles shared with the government, and (Annex 4) police detention review without a warrant in pilot districts. | **[FLAVOR]** |

### 6.3 Enemies: KOCH Secure

### 6.4 Civilians

**[FLAVOR]** Civilians include commuters, office staff, visitors, interns in navy Talent Program polos, analysts, cleaners and cooks. Before the alarm they only stare at the masked man. After it they cower, flee or raise their hands. Interns are the emotional centre of the crowd: they are SIGHT's unpaid workforce and also its training data.

**[OPEN #13]** Shooting a civilian: the proposal fails the mission instantly ("KOCH would like that."). The alternative is GTA-style consequences.

## 8. Central conflict and themes

**[FLAVOR]** **Central conflict:** one man with no power and a face the system already knows, against a corporation that can see everyone and is about to be given the whole city. Under the gunfight runs an inner choice: exposure or revenge.

| Theme | How the game carries it | Tag |
|---|---|---|
| **Being seen vs being watched** | The mask protects Arsen from the machine and accuses him in front of people. The opening refusal and the ending's "IDENTIFIED" form one frame: the same turnstile, refusing and then accepting the same face. | **[FLAVOR]** (the masked refusal itself is [CANON]) |
| **Faces as property** | FaceGate stores every pass, interns tag 400 faces an hour, and Arsen's own face is a "Watch" profile. A face is a key, a data point and a risk score. | **[FLAVOR]** |
| **Masks** | Arsen wears a literal mask. KOCH wears a figurative one: the PR demo, the "Safe City" slogans, the HR letter that turns a kidnapping into a "resignation". The game ends by asking who takes theirs off. | **[FLAVOR]** |
| **Unpaid youth as raw material** | The interns are the product's workforce *and* its training data, filmed while they work. | **[FLAVOR]** |
| **Ordinary people inside the machine** | Azamat, Nurai, Elmira, Meerim and the analysts are not villains. They are afraid or complicit, and the first person the player hurts is one of them. | **[FLAVOR]** |
| **Music vs surveillance** | The evidence is hidden "under a song", the only place nobody at KOCH would look. The hero's cover is a musician, and his weapon hides inside the instrument case. | **[FLAVOR]** (the guitar-case gun is [CANON]) |

## 9. Systems that carry the story

**[FLAVOR]** Each core system is designed to tell the story as well as to be played.

| System | Rule in brief | Story job | Tag |
|---|---|---|---|
| **KOCH ALERT meter** | 0 Calm, 1 Suspicious, 2 Search, 3 Combat, 4 Lockdown. Story beats raise it: the refusal → 1, Temirov's order → 2, the PC login → 3, the SIGHT shutdown → 4, which is permanent. | The campus "breathes" with the plot. The meter only appears at the FaceGate, the moment the machine notices Arsen. | **[FLAVOR]** |
| **Mask rule** | Masked: cameras flag "UNIDENTIFIED", and FaceGates refuse. Unmasked: SIGHT already knows his face, so a camera match means instant alert. There is no unmask button until the ending. | The mask is a mechanic and a theme. Taking it off is the game's final player action in ending A. | **[FLAVOR]** |
| **Guitar case holster** | The rifle stays hidden and Arsen reads as a musician. Drawing it takes 1.2 s, and civilians react only to a drawn rifle. | The [CANON] guitar-case gun as a play choice: hidden vs loud. | **[FLAVOR]** |
| **Stealth kit** | Pepper spray (3 charges), knife, stolen keycards, Azamat's radio (which hints where patrols are going), PA panels Arsen can hijack. | Every tool comes from Arsen's past or from the people he meets. | **[FLAVOR]** |
| **Evidence journal (20 collectibles)** | Documents, audio logs and SIGHT profiles (C01–C20), e.g. the HR resignation letter, "Profile: ARSEN", "Patient 4 chart", Arsen's unpaid AV invoice. | Optional backstory for players who look closer; the same files become the broadcast evidence. | **[FLAVOR]** |
