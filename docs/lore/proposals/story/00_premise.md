# KOCH-SIMULATOR: Premise

## Logline
A masked street musician walks into the headquarters of the corporation that "lost" his younger brother, carrying a rifle in a guitar case, and has one morning to find him before KOCH signs a contract that puts every face in the city under its watch.

## World
- **City:** an unnamed post-Soviet Central Asian capital. Trolleybus wires, Soviet apartment blocks, marshrutka-style buses, dust, poplars, a 4-lane avenue in front of the HQ.
- **Day:** one continuous day, the day of the contract signing. Story runs ~06:40 (wake) to ~12:15 (broadcast).
- **Time of year:** TODO_LORE (open question #2; story works in any season, clear morning assumed).
- **Mask type:** TODO_LORE (open question #3). **Azamat's fate:** proposal = wounded, survives (open question #4).

## KOCH
- A private corporation. Public face: "smart city" security (FaceGate turnstiles, CCTV analytics) and HR/talent services (recruiting, the Talent Program).
- **The name:** in-game KOCH is a brand only; the letters are never explained. No person in the game is called Koch.
- **HQ campus:** gate + checkpoint on the avenue; Block B (Visitor & PR Center, demo labs, Brew Point coffee shop); Block A (Executive HQ: CEO floor, Finance, HR, Legal, Archive, Clinic, Assembly Hall); Block C (Talent Program, analysts' attic, SIGHT server node in the basement); Block D (KOCH Bio, closed for this story).
- **Culture on the walls:** motivational posters, badge levels, "Clean Desk" rules, NDAs, a cheerful corporate PA voice.

## Project SIGHT (the secret)
| Part | What it is |
|---|---|
| FaceGate | The public product: turnstiles that open by face. Every pass is stored. |
| Annotation Cells | Talent Program interns tag faces from city cameras, 400 faces/hour, unpaid ("training"). |
| Baseline recording | The interns themselves are filmed in the training rooms (every room has CCTV) to train behaviour prediction: stress, lying, "agitation". |
| Risk profiles | Every identified citizen gets a SIGHT profile with a risk tier (Green / Watch / Refer). "Refer" = passed to city police for detention review. |
| City contract | Today Lind signs a city-wide contract: SIGHT on every public camera, profiles shared with the government. |

## Themes
- **Being seen vs being watched.** The mask is protection and accusation. The game ends on whether Arsen shows his face.
- **Unpaid youth as raw material.** The interns are the product's training data.
- **Ordinary people inside the machine.** Azamat, Nurai, the nurse, the interns: none are villains, all are complicit or afraid.
- **Exposure vs revenge.** Taking down "the company" (truth) vs "its people" (violence).

## Tone
- Grounded GTA-style crime action. Real guns, real wounds, real consequences (police, news).
- No sci-fi: SIGHT is cameras, servers, spreadsheets and contracts. No robots, no superpowers, no hacking minigames that look like movies.
- Dry humour from the corporate PA voice and posters; no jokes about Isa.
