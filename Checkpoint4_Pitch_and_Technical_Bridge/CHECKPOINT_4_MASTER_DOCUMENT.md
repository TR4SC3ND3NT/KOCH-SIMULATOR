# KOCH-SIMULATOR: Checkpoint 4 (pitch assets and technical bridge)

**Game:** story-driven first-person action sandbox in Unreal Engine 5.8.3. The setting is the headquarters campus of KOCH, a fictional evil corporation, recreated 1:1 from a real site. The hero wants to take the company down.

## Status
- **Done:** the campus (blocks A–D and a context city), import into UE, fixed ground, street furniture, a playable rifle prototype, a companion NPC prototype.
- **Not yet: characters.** The hero, the guard and staff are not in the build yet; they are coming soon. Mannequins and a prototype companion stand in for them.
- **Next:** characters, Block C interior, checkpoint turnstiles, main menu, dialogue.

All images are real frames from the UE 5.8 build (Lumen + Nanite, 1080p).
